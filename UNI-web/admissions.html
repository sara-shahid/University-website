Admissions

The University of Layyah welcomes applications from students seeking quality higher education in a variety of disciplines.

Admissions are offered on merit according to university rules and regulations.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Intermediate (FA/FSc/ICS/I.Com) or equivalent qualification.
  • Must meet the merit requirements of the selected program.
  • Required documents must be submitted before the deadline.

Required Documents

  • Matric Certificate
  • Intermediate Certificate
  • CNIC / B-Form Copy
  • Domicile Certificate
  • Passport Size Photographs

Admission Process

  1. Submit the admission application.
  2. Upload required documents.
  3. Merit list announcement.
  4. Fee submission.
  5. Registration and commencement of classes.
