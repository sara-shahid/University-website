About University of Layyah

Established: (as a sub-campus of Bahauddin Zakariya University)

University of Layyah is a public university located in Layyah,Punjab, Pakistan.We are leading a University in Pakistan,offering a wide range of academic programs.

The University of Layyah was Established in 2012 with the vision of providing quality education to the people of Layyah and surrondings areas.

Upgraded to full fledged University: 2022
Faculties:6(Arts, Science, Social Sciences, Commerce, and others )
Departments:23
Campuses:2(Main Campus on Karor Road and City Campus on Katchery Road)

Mission:To provide state-of-the-art education and promote research and development in various fields.

Academics

Offers a wide range of undergraduate , graduate , and doctoral programs in various fields , including business , enigneering , arts , and sciences.

  • Strong research focus, with opportunities for students to work with faculty on research projects.
  • Small class sizes , allowing for personal attention and mentorship.

Mission and Vision

Our mission is to provide a supportive and inclusive learning environment that fosters academic excellence , creativity , and innovation and community engagement . The university's vision is to become a leading institution of higher learning in Pakistan,recognized for its academic excellence,research and community service.

we value diversity , equity , inclusion and strive to create a community that is welcoming and respectful of all individuals.
