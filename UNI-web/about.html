About University of Layyah

University of Layyah is a public sector university located in Layyah, Punjab, Pakistan. It was established to provide quality higher education to students of the region and surrounding areas.

The university started as a sub-campus of Bahauddin Zakariya University and later became an independent university.

Today, the university offers undergraduate and postgraduate programs in various disciplines and continues to expand its academic and research activities.

Mission

To provide high-quality education, promote research, encourage innovation, and prepare students to become responsible citizens and professionals.

Vision

To become a leading institution of higher learning in Pakistan, recognized for academic excellence, research contributions, and community service.

Key Facts

  • Established as a BZU Sub-Campus in 2012
  • Upgraded to University Status in 2022
  • Multiple Academic Faculties
  • 23+ Academic Departments
  • Main Campus and City Campus
  • Research and Community Development Focus
